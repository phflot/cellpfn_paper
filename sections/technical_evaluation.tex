\paragraph{Frozen inputs and feature provenance.}
The ZEBRA subsample SHA256 is \texttt{428fe0a0}\allowbreak\texttt{64327cb1}\allowbreak\texttt{d513367e}\allowbreak\texttt{5bee2ab0}\allowbreak\texttt{8a1cf0a5}\allowbreak\texttt{ba93d40e}\allowbreak\texttt{9b219729}\allowbreak\texttt{9550ebd9}; the case builder checks this identity and the frozen evaluation-manifest identity before reusing its splits. The PBMC preparation joins exact source barcodes and retains the donor time codes without interpreting them as independently sampled subjects. Antibody quality-control totals in GEO metadata differed from the reproduction snapshot even though the audited antibody values and labels agreed; no protein-derived quantity entered inference. Feature selection is conditional on the labelled support matrix, except for the pre-existing kidney feature panels and the assay-availability mask explicitly described above.

For cortex count reconstruction, let $z_g=\exp(x_g)-1$ for a processed expression row and let $u$ be its smallest positive entry. Candidate counts were $z_g/u$. A row was rejected if any coordinate was more than 0.001 from an integer or if its reconstructed CP10k/log-transform round trip differed by more than $2\times10^{-6}$. Reconstruction preceded masking of unavailable genes. The 1,322 Yang Cortex cells failing this rule were excluded from eligible supports; existing support rankings were filtered, preserved in order and extended deterministically from the eligible original support pool. The original query cohorts were retained exactly. These checks support a conditional integer-lattice reconstruction; they do not convert inferred counts into independently observed raw reads.

\paragraph{Sex-task units and label resolution.}
The study-matched oligodendrocyte positive control required XIST and at least four of RPS4Y1, DDX3Y, UTY, KDM5D and EIF1AY to have been measured. Its 46 female--male unit pairs spanned eight studies; fourteen pairs entered each query split. The broader task added four Lim pairs and used sixteen query pairs. Common measured-gene universes were 16,708 and 12,668 genes, respectively, before support-selected 8,192-gene evaluation. Both tasks used the same four nested support-unit budgets, but 16 and 32 units supplied 160 and 320 cells per class, beyond the training support range. The joint change in studies and gene coverage is not a controlled removal of sex-linked genes. Means and ranges over reused-person splits are descriptive.

PBMC fine-task eligibility used the original donor quotas. Candidate tasks were retained when
\[
 \frac{R/100-1/K}{1-1/K}\geq0.20\quad\text{and}\quad R<85,
\]
where $K$ is the class count and $R$ is the larger mean balanced accuracy of nearest-class-mean and logistic-reference classifiers at 100 supports. These references used support-standardized 2,000-gene expression. The rule did not consult CellPFN/proxy outputs, but it did consult reference predictive performance and query labels. It therefore does not create an independent external test set. Nested labels and overlapping cells also prevent interpreting an eight-task mean as eight biological replicates.

\paragraph{Metric definitions.}
With $K$ query classes, class sizes $n_c$ and predictions $\hat y_i$, balanced accuracy was
\[
 \mathrm{BA}=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{c=1}^{K}\frac{1}{n_c}
             \sum_{i:y_i=c}\mathbf{1}(\hat y_i=c).
\]
Every declared class had to occur among the queries; the scorer rejects silent class dropping. Probabilities were obtained from class logits, and NLL was $-n^{-1}\sum_i\log\max(p_{i,y_i},10^{-12})$. ECE was $\sum_{j=1}^{15}(n_j/n)|\mathrm{accuracy}_j-\mathrm{confidence}_j|$, using confidence equal to the maximum class probability and bins $[(j-1)/15,j/15)$ for $j=1,\ldots,15$ with the final right edge included. Empty bins contributed zero. Reported BA is multiplied by 100; BA differences are percentage points. AUC remains on the zero-to-one scale.

For a paired diagnostic contrast over $D$ support draws, $d_s$ was computed within draw before averaging. The conditional 95\% interval was $\bar d\pm t_{0.975,D-1}s_d/\sqrt D$. This construction was used only where the analysis supplied a ten- or twenty-draw interval; it was not applied to the three-draw headline benchmark or reused-person sex controls. For the provisional ROSMAP analysis, the reported standard error used the Nadeau--Bengio correction\cite{nadeau2003inference}, $s\sqrt{1/10+n_{\mathrm{query}}/n_{\mathrm{support}}}$, where $s$ is the standard deviation over splits; the interval multiplier was 2.262. Its units are donors under the stated split design, not cells. The available aggregate export does not permit independent reconstruction of the underlying donor assignments or label corrections.

\paragraph{Conditional pool and operator updates for DS-LOO.}
For each query and its fixed predicted/runner-up classes, concatenate the pooled relation representations into $P$ and the additive operator pair embeddings into $h$, with fixed-pair margin $H(P,h)$. Holding query depth and detected fraction fixed, replace gene $g$'s relation vector $r_g$ by its zero-expression counterpart $r_g^0$. If $G$ genes are supplied, the changes in the componentwise mean and RMS are
\[
 \Delta P_g^{\mathrm{mean}}=\frac{r_g^0-r_g}{G},\qquad
 \Delta P_g^{\mathrm{RMS}}=
 \sqrt{\max\!\left(\frac{\sum_j r_j^2+(r_g^0)^2-r_g^2}{G}+\epsilon,0\right)}
 -\sqrt{\frac{\sum_j r_j^2}{G}+\epsilon}.
\]
Top-two maxima and their indices give the exact maximum after replacement. Writing $L=\log\sum_j\exp(r_j)$ componentwise, the normalized log-sum-exp change is
\[
 \Delta P_g^{\mathrm{LSE}}=
 \log\max\!\left[1-\exp(r_g-L)+\exp(r_g^0-L),10^{-30}\right].
\]
These replacements are exact conditional on the other relations and row-level scalars being fixed.

For the full operator, let $\Sigma$ denote the fitted covariance in equation~\ref{eq:operator-covariance}, restricted to support-variable coordinates, and let $v$ be the support-fitted inverse diagonal scale and
\[
 A=I-U\operatorname{diag}\!\left[1-(1+\lambda)^{-1/2}\right]U^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad w_c=A\{v\odot(z-\mu_c)\}.
\]
At fixed depth, setting gene $g$ to zero changes its standardized coordinate by $\delta_g=z_g^0-z_g$. DS-LOO updates the own-gene whitening coordinate as
\[
 w_{cg}^{(g)}=w_{cg}+A_{gg}v_g\delta_g,
\]
then applies the original clipping to $[-8,8]$, scaling by $1/4$ and support gate. Changes $A_{jg}v_g\delta_g$ in other coordinates $j\ne g$ are omitted. Thus the low-rank operator introduces an explicit approximation beyond head linearization.

The operator's scalar branch is updated separately. For every class $c$, its squared Mahalanobis distance changes according to
\[
 M_c^{(g)}=\max\!\left\{M_c+
 2\delta_g[\Sigma^{-1}(z-\mu_c)]_g+
 \delta_g^2[\Sigma^{-1}]_{gg},0\right\}.
\]
The implementation recomputes the original across-class centering, normalization, $\operatorname{asinh}$ transformation, gate-weighted recentering and gated scalar features from these distances, then reevaluates the nonlinear pair multilayer perceptron to obtain $\Delta h_g$. The resulting uncorrected approximation is
\[
 \widehat I_g^{\mathrm{conditional}}
 =-\nabla_P H(P,h)^{\mathsf T}\Delta P_g
  -\nabla_h H(P,h)^{\mathsf T}\Delta h_g.
\]
The second term is absent in the no-operator model. Pair-embedding reevaluation is exact for the conditional scalar inputs; the final scorer remains linearized. Selected-gene exact head replay evaluates $H(P+\Delta P_g,h+\Delta h_g)$ and therefore isolates head linearization while retaining the conditional-state approximations.

\paragraph{First-order depth and detection correction.}
Let $d=\max(\sum_jx_j,1)$ in count mode and $q=G^{-1}\sum_j\mathbf1(x_j>0)$. With expression held fixed, differentiate the reconstructed fixed-pair margin with respect to independent depth and detection leaves at the original query. The implemented corrected estimate is
\[
 \widehat I_g^{\mathrm{corrected}}
 =\widehat I_g^{\mathrm{conditional}}
 +\mathbf1_{\mathrm{count}}\frac{\partial m}{\partial d}x_g
 +\frac{\partial m}{\partial q}\frac{\mathbf1(x_g>0)}{G}.
\]
The depth derivative passes through the differentiable full query-side operator, including depth-induced changes to its whitening coordinates. It does not restore fixed-depth off-diagonal deletion effects. The correction is first order and does not eliminate nonlinear remainders or effects of clipping and the depth floor. Processed-mode inputs receive only the detected-fraction correction. Operator algebra uses float64, relation pools and head evaluation use float32, and TF32 is disabled.

The recovered historical consistency checks matched the reconstructed pooled representation and base operator channels exactly for all twelve case--model combinations. The largest recorded final-logit discrepancy was $1.04\times10^{-3}$ for the reference model and $1.53\times10^{-4}$ for the no-operator model. These checks are recorded execution diagnostics; they do not make the finite-deletion approximation exact.

Validation genes were drawn from unions of each method's class-level top twenty, the bottom twenty for integrated gradients and the operator readout, and twenty random expressed genes, augmented with previously untested DS-LOO top/bottom genes. DS-LOO scored every gene, while exact validation covered this selected union. The analysis compared gradient-times-input and integrated-gradients variants\cite{sundararajan2017ig} with the same finite-deletion target; numerical agreement is evidence of fidelity to that target, not a proof that the attribution methods estimate identical scientific quantities. For the principal corrected-DS-LOO comparison, Spearman correlations were computed across the original exact-deletion gene set for each cell, summarized by the median within each class and then the median over classes. Constant or undefined correlations were excluded by the recorded finite-value rule. Relative Euclidean error was computed within each class after flattening the gene-by-selected-cell arrays, $\|\widehat I-I\|_F/\|I\|_F$, and summarized by the median across classes. Additional DS-LOO-selected extreme genes augmented separately labelled uncorrected validation statistics; they did not augment the principal corrected statistic. Exact head replay and finite query deletion were kept distinct. Deletion-curve reversal denotes the original predicted-versus-runner-up margin becoming negative, not a recomputed global-argmax label change. Validation quantifies agreement with the specified finite-deletion target and supplies no analytical error bound or causal biological interpretation. The six cases include two representations of the same kidney cells and therefore are not six independent cohorts. Cell-level deletion curves and support-plus-query deletion experiments were kept separate because the latter refit the support statistics.
