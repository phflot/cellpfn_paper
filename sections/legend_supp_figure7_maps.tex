All queries are retained. Each case's RNA geometry was fitted once from its exact support/query union without labels: 2k HVGs, standardized expression clipped at ten standard deviations, at most fifty principal components, fifteen Euclidean neighbors and UMAP minimum distance 0.3, seed 42. Display preprocessing does not alter stored classifier inputs or outputs (Methods). Overlays within a case share coordinates; independently fitted cases have no common coordinate frame. These maps show RNA variation and model outputs, not CellPFN embeddings or independent cohort replication. Source data retain identities, coordinates and displayed values.
