\textbf{Illustrative RNA geometry of one predefined cellular-prior draw.} \textbf{m,n}, Identical unsupervised UMAP coordinates coloured by simulated cell type or donor. All 16,384 cells comprise eight donors, eight types and 256 cells per combination, with 8,192 anonymous genes. The mean-program bank (root 44; seed 42) was selected before generation. Log1p counts-per-10,000 and 2k HVGs supplied 50 principal components, 15 Euclidean neighbours and UMAP (minimum distance 0.3; seed 42). Labels did not determine geometry. This single draw is illustrative, not a realism assessment. Source Data provide coordinates, selected genes and the configuration receipt.
