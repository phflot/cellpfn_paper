\textbf{Supplementary Figure S7 continued: expression and signed deletion attribution.}
\textbf{i--l,} \textit{IL7R}/\textit{NKG7} query expression and corrected DS-LOO attribution in coarse PBMC (320 queries; 100 supports/class, draw 0).
\textbf{m--p,} \textit{PLP1}/\textit{CLDN5} overlays in development cortex (7,769 queries; ten supports/class, draw 0). Marker choices preceded map generation. PBMC expression is log1p counts per 10,000; cortex retains supplied processed values. Coordinates match panels \textbf{g,h} and the main biological figure.
Expression uses each gene's full observed range; attribution limits are symmetric around zero, independently per gene. Positive attribution means that the observed gene favors the predicted class over its fixed runner-up; negative values oppose that margin. Neither sign establishes regulation or causality.
Pale-gray attribution backgrounds mark unexplained queries; outlined colored points show all explained cells, including exact-zero scores, enlarged solely for visibility. Explanations cover 191/320 PBMC and 96/7,769 cortex queries, selected among correctly classified cells, and therefore do not characterize every query. Every query remains visible. RNA geometry and preprocessing are described in panels \textbf{a--h} and Methods; the renderer performs no additional normalization, embedding or model inference. Source data preserve cell identities, coordinates and displayed expression/attribution values.
