In the principal configuration the age-noise block is active, and the actual draw uses `k_eff_ig = clip(k_c_i,g / h_ig^2, 0.01, 160)`, where `h_ig` is the age-dependent noise multiplier. Use `k_eff_ig` in both gamma arguments and in the conditional-variance denominator; retain `k_cg` for the base dispersion curve. Condition the moments on the effective shape as well as library/profile. Evidence: `pyscintelligentdesign/src/pyScIntelligentDesign/data_models/mixed_program_task_distribution.py:4036–4053`; evaluated `76ba3a3`, same file `:3691–3708`; active block in `hashprompt/conf/foundation_realism_v2.yaml:80–85`, inherited by the principal configuration.

2. **Name the coordinate whose mean is preserved.** The age-noise sentence in `sections/methods_simulator.tex:33` and “mean preserved” in `sections/technical_simulator.tex:39` should say “preserves the expected cell-state log-shift.” The implementation preserves `E[u]` using `E[u] + h*(u-E[u])`; exponentiation and compositional normalization do not in general preserve mean expression. Likewise, subtype centring is centring of log offsets, not equality of average expression profiles. Evidence: `_expected_cell_shifts`, `mixed_program_task_distribution.py:2602–2644`, and application at `:3844–3850`; subtype mixture templates are explicitly recomputed after softmax at `:2688–2717`.
