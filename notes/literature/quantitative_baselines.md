# Original papers for quantitative annotation baselines

Verified 2026-10-14 with the citation-management and citation-verifier skills. This is targeted verification of three named original methods, not a systematic literature search. Searches used exact public titles in PubMed and cross-checked primary publisher or deposited author-manuscript records. No private manuscript material was submitted externally.

The local citation scan found no placeholders or duplicate keys before these additions. The DOI-to-BibTeX script could not resolve network hosts in the shell; web Crossref endpoints were also inaccessible. Records below were therefore extracted from the opened public source records and rendered with the citation-management `render_entry` helper. All authors are retained; CellTypist author given names remain the initials used by its PubMed and deposited author-manuscript records, without speculative expansion.

These citations identify methods, not the results of this benchmark. Local fitting choices, feature selection, defaults, training budgets and numbers remain governed by the export. In particular, citing an original method does not establish that the supplied implementation used its optimal configuration. Detailed code/configuration verification remains in Wave 2.

The three additions have verified titles, ordered author lists, years, journals, volumes, issues, pages or article numbers, and DOIs. No reference is a placeholder or an unpublished substitute for the original paper. A local bibliography audit checks syntax and key consistency separately from this source verification.
