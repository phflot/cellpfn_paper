## Search scope and access

This is a documented narrative review, not a systematic review or an exhaustive census. The local first pass inspected inherited bibliography entries and searched the seed/exports for model names. Public web discovery then targeted arXiv, PMLR, Nature/Science publisher pages, PubMed/PMC, and official model documentation. Primary-source records were retained; news, aggregator pages and social media were used only as leads and are not evidence. Backward searches followed PFN and TabICL references; forward name/title searches checked newer versions, biological PFNs and publication status. The machine-readable log is `pfn_search.json`.

The scholarly MCP advertised in the skill was not available. A direct public Crossref API request failed with a DNS error; web tools remained available. Some OpenReview and PMC direct opens returned browser challenges, and the Science publisher page was inaccessible. Those failures are recorded rather than described as full-text inspection. No private manuscript text was sent to search services. Metadata were transcribed from verified primary records, with published versions preferred over preprints and no guessed DOIs.

## Story synthesis and boundaries

The central opportunity is to specify a useful distribution of learning problems. The PFN objective already provides the bridge from a sampled task prior to an amortized predictor. CellPFN can therefore frame biological simulator design as a way of specifying its inductive bias, then test that prior in two settings: a compact purpose-built network and adaptation of independently developed tabular networks. The latter is evidence that the useful information is portable across architectures. This is the proposed interpretation of the project's experiments, not a conclusion proved by the external papers.

Cell count is an incomplete measure of biological coverage: scTab directly separates cell subsampling from donor subsampling and finds stronger scaling with donor diversity. That supports the inherited motivation about repeated cells versus varied experiments. It does not show that simulated donors reproduce all real donor variation. [Fischer et al., Results, “Cross-tissue cell type classification scales with dataset and model size”, Fig. 2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51059-5)

Avoid three overclaims. Synthetic training and cellular PFNs already have precedents; full-transcriptome cell models also exist; and modern cell foundation models can produce useful frozen embeddings. The narrower contribution is competitive support-conditioned classification from a cellular task prior, compact transcriptome-scale computation, and improvement of released general tabular learners with that same prior. This combined claim must be established by the current export and eventual independent evaluation.

### PFN foundations and general tabular models

**`muller2022pfn` — Müller et al., ICLR 2022.** Published conference paper; arXiv full text inspected, OpenReview browser challenge. Sections 2–3, equations (1)–(2), and Algorithm 1 define a prior over tasks, sample labelled datasets and optimize held-out predictive log loss. This supports calling the simulator a prior over data-generating processes/tasks and describing inference as posterior-predictive approximation under that prior. It does not prove that CellPFN's finite model is calibrated or that its prior represents the true biology. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.10510v7#S3); [conference record](https://openreview.net/forum?id=KSugKcbNf9).

**`hollmann2023tabpfn` — Hollmann et al., ICLR 2023.** Published conference paper; arXiv abstract and metadata inspected. Original TabPFN uses synthetic structural-causal-model priors and produces tabular predictions conditioned on labelled examples without parameter updates at inference. Retain for historical development if space permits; the 2025 paper is the more useful main-text foundation-model citation. Do not carry its original numerical feature limits over to later TabPFN versions. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01848); [conference record](https://openreview.net/forum?id=cp5PvcI6w8_).

**`hollmann2025tabpfn` — Hollmann et al., Nature 637, 319–326 (2025).** Published; full publisher text inspected. “Principled in-context learning” and Fig. 1 connect synthetic dataset generation with a learned predictive algorithm and explicitly identify the generator as the prior. Fine-tuning is already a stated model capability. Use for the synthetic-to-real tabular precedent; do not claim that continued training itself is novel. Its model is TabPFNv2, distinct from the project's TabPFN-3.5 comparator. [Primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08328-6).

**`qu2025tabicl` — Qu et al., ICML 2025, PMLR 267, 50817–50847.** Published; proceedings abstract and publisher-supplied BibTeX inspected. TabICL constructs fixed-width row representations, then performs in-context prediction, showing that generic tabular inference need not retain a full two-dimensional table throughout the entire network. Use as architectural context if needed; cite v2 for the actual comparator. Correct PMLR URL is `qu25d.html`, not `qu25a.html` (the latter is an unrelated paper). [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/qu25d.html).

**`qu2026tabiclv2` — Qu et al., ICML 2026, PMLR 306, 102477–102555.** Published; proceedings metadata and arXiv full text inspected. Section 4, “Three pretraining stages”, reports approximately 35 million datasets; Appendix B.1 lists 500,000 + 40,000 + 10,000 steps at batch size 64 (35.2 million scheduled dataset exposures). Section 7 finds interacting prior and architecture effects. These support exposure comparisons and the importance of task-distribution design, not matched-compute efficiency. Latest arXiv v2, September 2026, records ICML publication. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/qu26a.html); [training details](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11139v1#S4); [Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11139v1#A2).

**`nvidia2026kumo` — NVIDIA Kumo-Tabular 1.0.** Official model documentation, not a peer-reviewed paper; full model card inspected. “Release Date” gives 23 September 2026. “Training Dataset” reports about 35 million synthetic tables for small, 71 million for medium, and 137 million for large, generated from structural causal models. “Model Overview” gives approximately 28/62/215 million parameters; use the project's exact parameter counts for its evaluated checkpoints. This is the relevant tabular release, distinct from KumoRFM relational models. [Official card](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/kumo-tabular/modelcard); [weights and release metadata](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Kumo-Tabular).

**`jaeger2026tabpfn35` — Jäger et al., TabPFN-3.5 technical report (2026).** Preprint v2, 22 September 2026; arXiv metadata and full text inspected. Sections 3.1–3.3 describe the revised encodings, architecture and synthetic prior. Cite for the actual evaluated version, alongside the foundational 2025 reference if introducing the model family. No comparable total pretraining-task exposure was verified in this report; leave that comparison unspecified. [Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17895); [full text, model design](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17895v2#S3).

**`priorlabs2026tabpfn35card` — Prior Labs model card (2026).** Official live documentation, inspected 14 October 2026. “Checkpoints” lists the `20260909` checkpoints and the 20,000-feature guardrail; “Training Data and Priors” says training uses synthetic tabular tasks. The licensing section identifies non-commercial research/evaluation terms. Use for version/limit provenance and the export's required research-use notation. A software guardrail is not evidence of an intrinsic theoretical inability to process more genes. [Official card](https://huggingface.co/Prior-Labs/tabpfn_3_5).

### Closest biological PFNs

**`sextro2026mappfn` — Sextro, Kłos and Dernbach (2026).** arXiv preprint plus ICLR 2026 Gen² workshop paper; full arXiv text and workshop front matter inspected. Section 4 and Appendix B.2 train on a synthetic causal-perturbation prior using SERGIO; the real evaluation predicts differential expression under held-out perturbation contexts. Appendix B.2 uses 50-gene networks, while B.3 describes the real-data gene selection. Cite as a direct precedent for biological synthetic priors and gradient-free contextual adaptation. Its perturbation mapping task differs from CellPFN's label prediction; do not imply a head-to-head comparison. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21092v1); [workshop paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=jbllvmVD0W).

**`gao2026perturbpfn` — Gao, Hernández-Lobato and Guo (2026).** arXiv preprint; accepted at ICML 2026 FMSD workshop according to the primary record. Full text inspected. The abstract/method describe synthetic prior-predictive training of a structured graph/intervention model for chemical-response prediction. Section 5 explicitly limits mechanistic identifiability and transcriptome coverage. Cite alongside MapPFN to acknowledge established cellular PFNs. This work concerns interventions and causal structure; CellPFN's DS-LOO classifier attribution should not be presented as an equivalent capability. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23447); [discussion and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23447v1#S5).

### Cellular representation learning and motivation

**`theodoris2023geneformer` — Theodoris et al., Nature 618, 616–624 (2023).** Published; publisher abstract/metadata and indexed PMC author manuscript passages inspected. The abstract establishes pretraining on roughly 30 million single-cell transcriptomes, followed by task-specific fine-tuning; disease experiments provide a strong precedent for limited-data biological applications. Cite for empirical pretraining and transfer. This article's original corpus is not necessarily the corpus of the project's newer Geneformer checkpoint; use checkpoint-specific exposure records for overlap assessments. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06139-9); [author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10949956/).

**`cui2024scgpt` — Cui et al., Nature Methods 21, 1470–1480 (2024).** Published; publisher abstract, figure headings and metadata inspected, full article gated. The abstract states pretraining on over 33 million cells and downstream transfer to annotation, integration and perturbation tasks. Cite for empirical single-cell foundation modelling. Detailed tokenizer or computational-complexity claims are not established by the accessible abstract and should use the actual official implementation during Methods work. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02201-0).

**`hao2024scfoundation` — Hao et al., Nature Methods 21, 1481–1491 (2024).** Published; publisher abstract, data-availability section and PubMed metadata inspected. The model has 100 million parameters, covers approximately 20,000 genes and was pretrained on over 50 million human transcriptomes. Supports the scale and empirical-data dependence of representation pretraining. Full gene input coverage and the genes simultaneously processed by every attention component are different quantities; do not equate them. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02305-7); [PubMed 38844628](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38844628/).

**`rosen2026uce` — Rosen et al., Nature 656, 183–191 (2026).** Published; publisher-indexed abstract/main passages and PubMed metadata/Fig. 1–2 captions inspected. UCE learns a self-supervised representation that embeds new cells without model fine-tuning; Fig. 1 describes protein-derived gene embeddings. Its new-cell inference and cross-species applications prevent a blanket claim that prior empirical cell models always require retraining. Replace the inherited 2023 preprint citation. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10689-z); [PubMed 42420460](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42420460/).

**`pearce2026transcriptformer` — Pearce et al., Science 393(6806), aec8514 (2026).** Published; PubMed metadata and abstract inspected, Science full text inaccessible. The abstract describes models trained on up to 112 million cells from 12 species and disease-state/cell-type transfer. Use as a recent empirical representation-learning precedent, without unverified architectural detail. The PubMed issue date is 2 July; a Nature Index record gave 7 May. Retain the verified year/volume/article number and flag the day/month conflict rather than propagate it. Replace the inherited 2025 bioRxiv citation. [PubMed 42096520](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42096520/); [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aec8514).

**`fischer2024sctab` — Fischer et al., Nature Communications 15, 6611 (2024).** Published; publisher full-text search passages, Fig. 2 and PDF metadata inspected. Provides direct motivation for experiment/donor diversity and demonstrates that a supervised tabular architecture can exploit large cellular datasets. It is a useful conceptual comparison, not an evaluated comparator in the current export. [Primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51059-5).

**`bai2026sclong` — Bai et al., Nature Communications 17, 2380 (2026).** Published; full publisher text and PubMed metadata inspected. “scLong overview” and Fig. 1 establish an approximately billion-parameter model with transcriptome-scale context across 27,874 genes, pretrained on 48 million cells, with gene-ontology information. Use to bound any full-transcriptome novelty claim. The published author list has 16 names; the preprint has 17, so the bibliography follows the journal record. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69102-y); [PubMed 41639087](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41639087/).

The approximate 35 million versus 384,000 comparison is about 91-fold; 137 million versus 384,000 is about 357-fold. Prefer explicit counts in the main text over a potentially misleading single “training efficiency” multiplier.

## Bibliography hygiene and handoff

- Prefer the published TabICLv2, UCE and TranscriptFormer records; keep the precise arXiv full-text anchors in notes.
- The inherited `smith2024benchmark` record has generic authors and was not verified. It is excluded; it must not silently enter the active bibliography.
- Retained staging references contain no placeholder keys or invented page numbers/DOIs. Model cards are identified as documentation; workshop/preprint status is visible.
- Inference-speed claims require project timings with matched hardware, preprocessing and ensemble settings. Literature speed claims describe their own experiments and cannot be transferred to CellPFN.
- Single-cell cell counts and synthetic task counts are different units; no numeric “less data than Geneformer” ratio is justified.
- Broader benchmarking criticism, datasets, simulator families and attribution methods are covered in the other literature/evidence notes rather than duplicated here.
