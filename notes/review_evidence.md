# Independent evidence review of the first Wave 1 draft

Reviewed `sections/abstract.tex`, `sections/results.tex`, the introduction's framing and the unresolved-claim macro in `main.tex` against the harmonized export and registries. This is an evidence review, not a bibliography/full-text citation audit. The original export was not modified. The numeric checker passed 58 local extractions/calculations with zero failures; it correctly excluded verification of the external TabICLv2 source and marked the exposure ratio as externally dependent.

## Corrections that matter to interpretation

1. **Distinguish a shared simulator from identical prior exposure.** Abstract line 1 says “The same prior improved three pretrained tabular models”; Results paragraphs P-R01/P-R03/P-R09 make the training story read as if one unchanged prior produced both results. CellPFN G1v2′ used v1 for the first 320,000 scheduled episodes and v2.2-B for the final 64,000; tabular continued training used v2.2-B throughout its cellular exposure. Add a compact version-lineage sentence near P-R03 or P-R09 and use “the cellular prior” or “the same simulator” where exact equality is not intended. Do not attribute all CellPFN performance to the v2.2-B realism properties. Sources: `data/fig1__lineage.csv`, G1v2′ row; `FIGURES.md:393–400` and `1704–1712`; `data/fig3__arms.csv`.

2. **Name the concrete pretraining exposure in the benchmark qualification.** P-R06 currently says only that comparisons combine “different pretraining exposure”. Both Geneformer and scGPT were very likely pretrained on the exact PBMC cells; Geneformer also encountered source studies contributing most cortex OOD query cells. Add one short sentence stating this. Otherwise “held-out studies” may be read as held out from every model's pretraining. Preserve “likely”, and do not attribute their performance to exposure or call it label leakage. Sources: `FIGURES.md:950–958`, `971–981`; `data/supp_exposure__figure_marks.csv` and `supp_exposure__study_exposure.csv`.

3. **Make the real-informed prior boundary explicit.** P-R01 correctly says aggregate real statistics informed ranges, and P-R06 correctly calls the benchmark development panels. Connect these facts: some of the same real datasets used for evaluation supplied simulator-calibration summaries, and earlier architecture/design choices used their results. A short qualification in one place is sufficient. The current text does not falsely claim fully independent validation, but readers should not need to infer the dataset overlap. Sources: `fig1__facts.csv` keys `sim.real_data_role`, `sim.real_data_leak_caveat`, `training.simulated_only.design_choices_caveat`.

## Precise metric and wording fixes

- **P-R13:** define the explained margin explicitly as the initially predicted class logit minus the runner-up logit. This establishes that DS-LOO timings describe two targets per explained cell, not all class outputs, and gives the following deletion experiment its reference. “DeepSets leave-one-gene-out” is the export's expansion of DS-LOO. The fixed-support/fixed-covariance, linearized-head and correction description is otherwise accurate. Source: `FIGURES.md:1059–1089`.
- **P-R14:** replace “corrected magnitude errors” with “corrected relative L2 errors”. The 0.5–4.9% range consists of case-level summaries, computed per class then median over classes, for G1v2′. Its label is not an absolute probability error. Rank-correlation wording correctly specifies case medians, but can say “median over cells and then classes” if space allows. Source: registry R28–R31 and `FIGURES.md:1124–1130`.
- **P-R16:** state that the supplied corrected repeated-split interval is **95%**. This is a direct source field, not a new analysis. The exact AUC and limits, matching within sex, donor-level interpretation and whole-paragraph red status are correct. Source: R38–R40; `supp_rosmap__auc.csv` field `interval_type`.
- **Abstract:** approximately 90-fold fewer is arithmetically supported, but TabICLv2 reports synthetic *datasets*, not a field literally called episodes. Prefer “approximately 90-fold fewer training tasks than TabICLv2's reported pretraining exposure” while retaining “384,000 scheduled synthetic episodes”. Results P-R03 already defines the differing units and excludes a matched-compute interpretation. Do not compare 35 million with 12,000 banks.
- **P-R12:** “Changing which simulated tasks received training weight” may imply simple reweighting of an unchanged stream. Prefer “Changing the simulated task distribution”: GL1 changes target families/lane eligibility and therefore the number of cells per update. The calibration tradeoff and confound are correctly stated. Source: `FIGURES.md:1323–1346`, `1579–1611`.
- **P-R12:** “prominent marker effects were reproduced more closely than the difficulty” is a qualitative comparison across statistics with different units. Clearer is “the prior covered the observed strongest-marker range, while its few-cell discrimination tasks were easier than the real reference”. This is the documented partial-fidelity result, not a new aggregate realism metric. Source: `FIGURES.md:1635–1702`.

## Red-marker coverage and remaining apparatus

The unresolved numerical ROSMAP paragraph is entirely enclosed in `\unresolvedclaim{O02}{...}`. The unperformed disease-signature/independent-cohort work is red under O03, and the aspirational abstract outcome is red under O04. The future frozen external evaluation appears under O01. `main.tex` defines this macro outside the optional draft-notes switch, so these claims cannot disappear from view by toggling ordinary comments.

The numeric benchmark descriptions themselves need not all turn red: they are completed developmental measurements and are qualified accordingly. Similarly, explicitly labelled extrapolated or shared-GPU runtime estimates can remain as accurately bounded observations. Their limits should not be silently promoted to definitive timing or final-validation results in the abstract.

At the time of this review, `notes/open_claims.md`, referenced by the draft's title-page note, had not yet been created. Before handoff, ensure O01–O04 have entries identifying what evidence resolves them, and that compiling the manuscript leaves the complete provisional prose visibly bold red. Kumo pretraining counts remain intentionally unavailable in the exposure registry pending integration of the other agent's primary-modelcard verification; no unsupported Kumo task-count claim appears in this draft.
