# NBT Article working constraints

Target remains Nature Biotechnology. Official [content-type guidance](https://www.nature.com/nbt/content), checked 2026-10-14: Article main text up to3,000 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and legends; abstract up to150 words without references; up to6 display items; approximately50 references as a guideline. Introduction has no heading; Results and Methods have topical subheadings; Discussion has none.

Working allocation: introduction approximately550 words, Results approximately1,850, reserve approximately600 for Discussion. Actual counts are generated by `make words` and saved in `output/review/word_counts.json`. Scientific prose inside red claims counts toward these budgets; marker IDs and editorial apparatus do not.

The active layout is an editable article-class review draft with numbered superscript citations, not a claim of final publisher typesetting. Bibliographic formatting and submission declarations can be finalized after Methods and figures. Novelty is the demonstrated value of a cellular task prior through compact inference and cross-architecture adaptation. Donor-aware simulation, biological PFNs and transcriptome-scale models have existing precedents. NBT's biological utility case remains dependent on completing the explicitly provisional application evidence; journal acceptance is not implied.
